3D & Motion Designer

Adibul Jabir

Since I was a kid, I have always been interested in technology and design. My journey started in Class 9 when I discovered Blogspot, HTML and CSS. I created and designed my own website and explored different web features. Later, I moved into graphic design, creating logos, banners and visuals. Then I explored 3D design and motion design using Blender & After Effects, working on modeling, animation, advertisements and explainer video. After that, I moved into UI/UX design, which connects well with my CSE background. I am still working in this field today. Currently, I focus on minimal design and want to combine 3D with front-end development so I can bring my designs to life without developer handoff.
